# Business Problem

In this project, our team worked with a manager of the credit card division at a bank. The client was concerned about an increasing number of customers leaving the bank's credit card division. The client reached out to our team for help identifying customers at risk of canceling their credit card services at the bank.

The client provided us with a random sample of ~10,000 customers' data in a `*.xlsx` file. The data contained characteristics of each customer and whether they churned or not.

The credit card management team would reach out to customers we identified as being at risk of churning with offers to try to get them to revert their decision to cancel their services at the bank.
