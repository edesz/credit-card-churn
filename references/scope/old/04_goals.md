# Goals

*What are the project goals?*

The goal of this project is to reduce the rate of customers who are likely to churn during **a certain period in the future**.

At-risk customers are those showing signs of likely canceling their credit card services (churning) in the future. The project's goal can be acheived by proactively identifying and characterizing at-risk customers, in order to efficiently target them in an attempt to revert their decision.
