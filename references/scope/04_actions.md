# Actions Being Informed by Project Goal

*What are the actions to be taken to address the goal (problem of reducing the rate of customer churn)?*

## Actions

### Identification of the Action

The intervention, or action, is to proactively contact (target) customers at risk of churn during a **certain period in the future** in order to get them to reverse their decision before the end of **that period**.

In addition to identifying the at-risk customers, characterizing (or profiling) them allows the client to know how to target them. This will allow the client to implement data-driven proactive interventions at the at-risk customers.

### How is the Goal Informed by Action?

By proactively targeting at-risk customers, the client can reduce the increasing churn rate and improve customer loyalty.

### Client that will Execute this Action

As mentioned earlier, the client is the manager of credit card division. The client will perform the targeting in collaboration with the management team of the credit card division.

### Who is the Recipient of this Action?

The existing credit card customers who are predicted to be at risk of canceling their credit card services would be the recipient of this action.

### What is the Frequency of Executing this Action?

At-risk clients will be contacted one or more times during a **certain period in the future**. The exact number of future periods is not known but it is at the discretion of the client.

### Which Channels will be Used to Execute this Action?

All channels available to the bank will be deployed

1. in-person
2. digital
3. telephone

### Are there any Resource or Capital Constraints? If so, what are they?

At this time resource or capital constraints are not known. So, recommendations should propose two scenarios

1. target sub-group of at-risk customers that efficiently maximize a business metric
2. target all at-risk customers

(ethics)=
### What are the Ethical Issues Associated With this Action?

We assume the bank complies with industry constraints and policies when targeting at-risk customers.

If allowed by performance, we will not use sensitive attributes in the data (age, gender, marital status, etc.) to identify the at-risk customers that are targeted with compeling retention offers (loyalty incentives).

(useful-info)=
### Is there any Other Useful Info about this Action?

We assume

1. targeting will be performed using the proactive outputs of a predictive model that can only be evaluated after the outcome (churn or no churn) of the action is known
2. any required approvals before acting are internal to the bank
3. The length of time before this action bears fruit is variable. Some customers will revert (from churning) immediately, while others will take time (days or weeks) before reverting.
